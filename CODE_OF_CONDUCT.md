# Community code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience. Critique evidence, behavior, and software rather than a person's identity or ability. A relationship shown by this tool is a reason to investigate, not a reason to harass an author.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and intentional sharing of private information are unacceptable. Do not publish private source corpora or identify individuals from confidential material.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation with proportionate explanations. For a conduct concern, open an issue containing only a request for private contact. Do not include personal details publicly. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
